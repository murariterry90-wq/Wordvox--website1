    M-Pesa number
    1. Open M-Pesa and choose Send Money.
    2. Enter the number above.
    3. Enter the amount for your hire, then your PIN.
    4. Copy the M-Pesa confirmation code and enter it in the form.
    PayPal account
    Open PayPal
    1. Log in to PayPal and choose Send.
    2. Enter the email address above.
    3. Enter the amount for your hire and put your name in the note.
    4. Copy the PayPal transaction ID and enter it in the form.
